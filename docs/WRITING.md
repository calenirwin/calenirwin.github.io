# Writing rhythm

Start with one article every two weeks. Treat this as a working plan, not an automatic publication schedule.

The next draft is **The work around the model**, in `content/blog/the-work-around-the-model.md`. It explores practical questions around AI adoption without attributing unverified outcomes or stories to the author. It is unpublished and needs an author edit before release.

## A small repeatable cycle

- Week one: choose one question, collect a few notes, and write a rough draft.
- Week two: add a concrete example you are comfortable sharing, remove anything you cannot substantiate, read it aloud, and edit in Pages CMS.
- Publish when the piece is ready. Check the deployed article and RSS feed afterward.

For the next draft, replace general passages with your own observations where useful. Avoid confidential workplace examples. Ask a reader one specific question at the end if you want a conversation to follow.

Possible follow-ups:

- What rebuilding Hnefatafl teaches about testing an AI experiment.
- Why an easy editing workflow matters more than another site redesign.
- How to decide whether an AI answer is useful enough to act on.

## Draft review

Open Blog in Pages CMS and keep Published off while editing. The production build and local site both exclude unpublished content, including RSS and sitemaps. Review the draft in the editor or its Markdown source. There is no public draft-preview deployment.

Dates label posts; they do not schedule them. Turning Published on and saving triggers deployment immediately.
